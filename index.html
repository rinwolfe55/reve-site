Wear & Where

Your closet's more fun with friends.

Put together outfits from the clothes you already own, then show them to the friends whose opinion you actually want. They can heart it, help you pick between two looks, or just tell you hmm.

Coming soon to theApp Store iPhone · free

There's no public feed, no strangers, no follower count. Just you and the few friends who actually get your style.

What you can do

Made for you and a few close friends.

1

Build outfit moodboards

Drag your actual clothes around on a colored canvas until the outfit feels right. Save the ones you love and see how the pieces come together.

2

Just your friends

Send a look to everyone or just one or two people. No public feed, no strangers — your closet stays yours.

3

Real opinions

Your friends react with a heart or a thumbs-down — and when you can't choose between two outfits, post both and let them settle it.

4

Know what you own

Snap photos to fill out your closet, sort it into tops, bottoms, and the rest, and keep track of what you actually reach for.
